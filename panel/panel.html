    Add links

    Paste anything: one link, a whole Reddit comment, a column copied from a spreadsheet. Product links are picked out and everything else is ignored. Other agents' links (CNFans, Kakobuy, Pandabuy…) are converted.

    Works when the tab is a product page (Weidian, Taobao, 1688, a Yupoo album or another agent's listing).

    Opening listings

    Use {url} for the encoded product link, or {id} and {platform} (weidian / taobao / 1688).

        More sites

        RepHop runs on Reddit, Yupoo, Google Sheets and the shopping sites. Add another site (a Discord web page, a finds site) and it will show the same buttons there.

          Privacy and clean-up

          Besides your list, RepHop remembers which links you opened, which posts a link appeared in, and previews it has already looked up. All of it stays in this browser.

          How RepHop works

          1. Browse Reddit as usual. Every Weidian, Taobao, 1688 and Yupoo link, and every other agent's link, gets a small button next to it.
          2. Click the link (or the ⚡ button) and the listing opens in Superbuy, in one tab that is reused, so you never pile up tabs.
          3. Click ＋ to save a find here instead. Go through them later, one at a time, with Go through.

          Good to know

          • Yupoo albums: RepHop reads the album and uses the Weidian or Taobao link the seller put in the description. If there is none, the album is saved as-is so you can message the seller.
          • Hover a link (or focus it with the keyboard) to see the picture, name and price before opening anything. Weidian and Yupoo previews load directly; Taobao and 1688 have no public preview, so theirs fill in after you have opened the listing once.
          • Save all links in the corner bar saves every link on the page plus the comments Reddit has not put on screen yet. Reddit hands over about 500 comments at a time; on bigger threads RepHop tells you how many were left out.
          • Google Sheets: the corner bar can grab the links from the tab you are on or from all tabs. “Clickable view” opens a plain version of the sheet where every link gets its own buttons.
          • Right-click any product link, a text selection or the page for the same actions on any website.
          • Check availability (More menu) flags Weidian items that have sold out or been removed, and Yupoo albums that were deleted. Taobao and 1688 can only be checked by opening them.
          • The reused tab is only reused while it still shows a listing. If you have moved on in it (cart, checkout, another site), RepHop leaves it alone and opens a fresh one.
          • Stay signed in to your agent: Superbuy only shows a listing when you are logged in.
          • Privacy: your list and history stay in this browser. RepHop has no server and collects nothing. It contacts Weidian and Yupoo (pages and pictures) for previews, follows short links you hover, open or save, reads the Reddit thread or Google Sheet you ask it to grab, and asks a public rate service for the yuan exchange rate. Clear the history any time under Settings.
