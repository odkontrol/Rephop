    Add links

    Paste anything: one link, a whole Reddit comment, a column copied from a spreadsheet. Product links are picked out and everything else is ignored. Other agents' links (CNFans, Kakobuy, Pandabuy…) are converted.

    Works when the tab is a product page: Weidian, Taobao, 1688, a Yupoo album, or a listing on one of the built-in agents.

    Opening listings

    Use {url} for the encoded product link, or {id} and {platform} (weidian / taobao / 1688).

    To reuse one tab on this site, RepHop has to be able to see that tab. Without it, each listing opens in a new tab.

      Trusted sellers

      Their links get a gold outline on Reddit. Add a Yupoo username here, or tick “trusted” on any saved item (for Weidian items the shop is looked up a little while after you save them).

        Add to cart for me

        Select items in your list and press Add to cart. RepHop opens each one at your agent and, on Superbuy and AllChinaBuy, sets the options and presses Add To Cart. To choose, it uses in this order: what you picked on that listing before, the size or colour written on the item (under Edit), then your sizes below. It only picks what it has a reason to pick: if a choice is sold out, missing, or RepHop simply has nothing to go on, it presses nothing and leaves that listing to you, then carries on once you have added it. On other agents it opens each listing and you do the choosing. Nothing is ever paid for: items only go into your cart.

        My sizes

        Write sizes the way sellers do. Separate alternatives with commas (“43, 9.5”): the first one found on the listing is used. Tops covers shirts, hoodies and jackets; bottoms covers pants and shorts.

        More sites

        RepHop runs on Reddit, Yupoo, Google Sheets and the shopping sites. Add another site (a Discord web page, a finds site) and it will show the same buttons there.

          Privacy and clean-up

          Besides your list, RepHop remembers which links you opened, which posts a link appeared in, and previews it has already looked up. All of it stays in this browser.

          How RepHop works

          1. Browse Reddit as usual. Every Weidian, Taobao, 1688 and Yupoo link, and every other agent's link, gets a small button next to it.
          2. Click the link (or the ⚡ button) and the listing opens in your agent, in one tab that is reused, so you never pile up tabs.
          3. Click ＋ to save a find here instead. Go through them later, one at a time, with Go through, or select some and press Add to cart to have RepHop put them in your agent's cart.

          Good to know

          • Yupoo albums: RepHop reads the album and uses the Weidian or Taobao link the seller put in the description. If there is none, the album is saved as-is so you can message the seller.
          • Hover a link (or focus it with the keyboard) to see the picture, name and price before opening anything. Weidian and Yupoo previews load directly; Taobao and 1688 have no public preview, so theirs fill in after you have opened the listing once.
          • Save all links in the corner bar saves every link on the page plus the comments Reddit has not put on screen yet. Reddit hands over about 500 comments at a time; on bigger threads RepHop tells you how many were left out.
          • Google Sheets: the corner bar can grab the links from the tab you are on or from all tabs. “Clickable view” opens a plain version of the sheet where every link gets its own buttons.
          • Already on your agent's site? The bar in the corner of a listing has Save to RepHop; so does a right-click on the page, Alt+Shift+K, and the strip at the top of this list. Saving there also remembers the colour and size you had picked.
          • Add to cart for me: on Superbuy and AllChinaBuy, RepHop sets the options from what you chose before and from your sizes (Settings), presses Add To Cart once and waits for the site to confirm. If a choice is sold out, missing, or it has nothing to go on, it presses nothing and asks you on the page. On other agents it opens each listing in turn and marks it when you have added it yourself. Quantities above 1 are set on Superbuy and AllChinaBuy only.
          • Deleting: the × on a card deletes it (with Undo). Select all, then Delete, clears a whole folder or the whole list.
          • Right-click any product link, a text selection or the page for the same actions on any website.
          • Check availability (More menu) flags Weidian items that have sold out or been removed, and Yupoo albums that were deleted. Taobao and 1688 can only be checked by opening them.
          • The reused tab is only reused while it still shows a listing. If you have moved on in it (cart, checkout, another site), RepHop leaves it alone and opens a fresh one.
          • Stay signed in to your agent: Superbuy only shows a listing when you are logged in.
          • Privacy: your list and history stay in this browser. RepHop has no server and collects nothing. It contacts Weidian and Yupoo (pages and pictures) for previews, follows short links you open or save, reads the Reddit thread or Google Sheet you ask it to grab, and asks a public rate service for the yuan exchange rate. A website can tell RepHop's buttons are on its page, but not which links you saved, viewed or trusted. Clear the history any time under Settings.
